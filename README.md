# 💊 Pharmacology Study App

Interactive study material for pharmacology, chapters 1–33 (Katzung-based):

- **Learn** – big picture, drug tables with short + step-by-step mechanisms, exam traps, memory hooks
- **Flashcards** – 332 cards with an "Again" pile
- **Quiz** – 138 multiple-choice questions with explanations
- **Anaesthesia** – how each drug is used in anaesthesia, grouped by role
- **Compare** – receptors, diuretics, antiarrhythmics, antidotes, teratogens, PK formulas

👉 **Open it:** https://raguzluka.github.io/pharmacology-cram/

Progress is saved in your own browser only.

Based on pharmacology study notes by Anđela V. (Katzung, *Basic & Clinical Pharmacology*, 14th ed.). For study purposes only – not medical advice.
